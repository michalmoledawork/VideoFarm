VideoFarm Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VideoFarm handles information when you connect social media accounts and use the application.

1. Information used by VideoFarm

Depending on the services you connect, VideoFarm may process account profile information, authentication tokens, basic account statistics, video files, captions and upload status information.

2. TikTok information

When you connect a TikTok account, VideoFarm may receive and store access tokens, refresh tokens, basic profile information and account statistics that you authorize through TikTok. These are used only to provide account connection, upload, status and analytics features inside VideoFarm.

3. Video files and captions

VideoFarm processes videos and captions that you create or select for upload. These files are used for the functions you request, including preparing and sending content to connected platforms.

4. Storage

VideoFarm is primarily a desktop application. Configuration data and connected-account credentials may be stored locally on the user's computer so the application can remain connected between sessions.

5. Sharing of information

VideoFarm does not sell personal information. Information is sent to third-party platforms only when required to perform features that you authorize, such as authenticating an account or uploading a video.

6. Third-party services

Connected platforms process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. VideoFarm does not control those third-party services.

7. Security

Reasonable steps should be taken to protect access tokens and credentials. Users should not share authentication tokens or secret keys publicly.

8. Your choices

You can stop using a connected account by removing its credentials from VideoFarm and revoking the application's access in the relevant third-party service.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when VideoFarm features or integrations change.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the VideoFarm operator through the contact details provided with the application.
